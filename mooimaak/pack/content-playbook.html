Content playbook

Decor sells on sight. Your marketing is one thing done every week: show a space before, show it after, say what it cost. This is where to post, what to post, and the words to use for the first 90 days.

2. Account setup, step by step

  1. Create a Gmail address for the business and use it for every account below.
  2. Instagram: create the account, switch to a Business profile, category "Interior design studio".
  3. Instagram bio: "We make it beautiful. Homes · Nurseries · Events. Cape Town. Packages from R1,200. Quote in a day ↓" then your website link.
  4. Create three Highlights with covers: Homes, Nurseries, Events. Add a fourth, Prices, with one Story per package.
  5. Facebook: create a Page, link it to Instagram in Meta Business Suite so one post goes to both.
  6. TikTok: create a Business account with the same name, photo and bio.
  7. Google Business Profile: register as a service-area business (no shop address shown), areas: Cape Town, Southern Suburbs, Northern Suburbs, Stellenbosch. Add services and from-prices.
  8. Pinterest: Business account, claim your website, make boards: Nursery ideas, Lounge makeovers, Baby shower decor, Party tables, Small wedding decor.
  9. WhatsApp Business: add catalogue items for all nine packages with prices; set an away message and a greeting; save quick replies for "prices", "areas" and "deposit".
  10. Join 10 Facebook groups: your suburb groups, Cape Town moms groups, Cape Town wedding and party supplier groups. Read each group's advertising rules first.
  11. Put the website link in every bio and the quote link as the Instagram action button.

How to film a transformation, every time

  1. Before you touch anything: 5 seconds of video from the doorway, phone upright, lights as they are.
  2. During: three 3-second clips of hands doing something (hanging, folding, placing).
  3. After: the same doorway shot, same height, lights on, curtains open.
  4. One detail shot and, if they agree, the client's reaction.
  5. Text on screen in the first second: what it is and what it cost. Example: "R4,500 lounge makeover, Plumstead".

Always get written permission (a WhatsApp "yes" is enough) before posting a client's home, child or guests. Never show a house number or street.

Paid advertising

Do not pay for ads until you have 9 posts and 3 reviews. Then spend R2,000 a month:

  • R1,200 boosting your best transformation Reel on Instagram and Facebook. Audience: women 25 to 45 within 25 km of your base. Button: Send WhatsApp message.
  • R800 on a nursery Reel aimed at interests "pregnancy" and "baby shower", same area.
  • Measure one thing: WhatsApp enquiries per R100 spent. Stop any ad that brings fewer than one.

7. Offline, because decor is local

  • Visit five antenatal class instructors and three baby shops with cards and a one-page nursery price list.
  • Visit ten estate agencies with a before-and-after print of a staged room.
  • Meet five venues without an in-house decorator; ask to go on their supplier list.
  • Take a small stand at one baby expo or market in the first 90 days verify stand cost.
  • Leave a small card on every event table: "Styled by Mooimaak" with a QR code to the site.
