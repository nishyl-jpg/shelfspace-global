1. The business in one paragraph

Mooimaak styles homes, sets up newborn nurseries and decorates events for mid-market Cape Town households, at fixed package prices from R1,200 to R25,000. The three lines share one customer: a nursery client becomes a baby shower client, then a birthday client. Home and nursery work earns 68% to 94% gross margin because it sells skilled time. Events earn 40% to 50% because the business rents in the tables, chairs, linen and backdrops it does not yet own.

2. Why it works

  • Low cost to start, early income. The founder launches on R24,000 of own capital. The business breaks even at three jobs a month.
  • Transparent pricing in a "DM for price" market. Published packages remove the main reason people do not enquire.
  • Repeat by design. Family milestones come every year. Each client is worth several jobs.
  • Counter-seasonal lines. Events peak October to March; home and nursery work fills winter and weekdays.
  • A clear use for capital. Owning hire stock lifts margin on every event and creates a second income from hiring it out.

6. Why this split is fair to both

  • The investor carries the cash risk, so the investor is repaid first, is secured by stock that can be resold, and keeps 20% of a going business afterward.
  • The founder carries the work, so the founder earns a capped salary from day one, keeps control, and keeps 80% of everything after repayment.
  • Half of profit stays in the company, so neither side strips the business before it has grown.
  • The buy-back gives both an exit: the founder can regain full ownership; the investor is guaranteed a floor price if that happens.

8. Risks an investor should weigh

  • Key person. The business depends on the founder. Mitigation: documented job checklists, a trained second stylist, the stock as security.
  • Demand is discretionary. In a tight economy people decorate less. Mitigation: entry packages from R1,200 and low overheads of about R6,500 a month.
  • No trading history yet. Mitigation: raise only after the first 12 weeks of results.
  • Stock damage and theft. Mitigation: insurance, damage clause in client terms, storage unit.
  • Seasonality. Mitigation: three lines with different seasons.

9. What I am asking you for

R150,000, a seat at a quarterly review, and introductions to people who host, sell houses or run venues. In return: first claim on payouts until you are repaid, 20% of the company, and clear numbers every quarter.
