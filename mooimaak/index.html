Questions

Good to know

What does the package price include?

Our time: the visit, the design, the shopping and the styling day. Items we buy or hire for you are charged at what we paid, with the slips.

Can you work with what I already have?

Yes. Most room refreshes keep the big pieces and change paint, lighting, textiles and layout. It is the cheapest way to make a room feel new.

When should I book a nursery?

Around weeks 20 to 28 is ideal, so furniture orders arrive and the room is finished about a month before the due date.

How far ahead do events need to be booked?

Small parties: two weeks. Larger celebrations: six to eight weeks. Peak season, October to March, books up earlier.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

50% books your date and covers sourcing; the balance is due before the styling or event day. Cancel within five business days of booking with no penalty; after that we charge only for time and items already spent.

Which areas do you cover?

Greater Cape Town and the Winelands. Travel beyond 40 km of the city centre is quoted per trip.
