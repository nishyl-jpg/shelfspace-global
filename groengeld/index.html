    How it works

    Four steps, then it runs itself

    1. Build your order

      Pick items and days above and send it on WhatsApp. We confirm your first delivery date.

    2. We buy at dawn

      Every delivery morning we buy to order at the market, so nothing sits in a store room.

    3. We deliver

      Kitchens before 10:00. Households between 9:00 and 14:00, with a message when we are close.

    4. You pay weekly

      Households pay by EFT before delivery. Restaurants get one invoice a week.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Why do prices change?

      Market prices move with the weather and the season. Household box prices are fixed; we adjust what goes in. Restaurant prices are set every Sunday and held for that week.

      What if something arrives bruised or short?

      Send a photo on WhatsApp the same day. We credit it on your next delivery, no argument.

      Is there a contract or a minimum term?

      No. Change, pause or cancel any time before 18:00 the day before a delivery.

      Is there a delivery fee?

      Where does the produce come from?

      The Cape Town Market in Epping, where farmers from across the Western Cape and beyond sell every morning, and directly from local growers as we grow.

      Is it organic?

      Not by default. It is good, fresh, conventional produce at fair prices. Tell us if you need organic items and we will quote.
