How we choose

Chosen by hand, every morning

What we look for in every item, how it gets from the market floor to your door, and how to ask for exactly what you want.

What we look for

Six checks before anything goes in your crate

We buy by hand at the Cape Town Market. If an item fails one of these, it stays on the floor.

Colour

Bright, even colour for its kind. No dull, grey or yellowing patches.

Firmness

Firm to a gentle squeeze. Nothing soft, wrinkled or spongy.

Skin and leaves

Clean, unbroken skin. Crisp leaves and green stalks, no slime or wilting.

Size and shape

Even sizes in a pack, so everything cooks at the same speed.

Smell and ripeness

Smells fresh at the stem. Ripe for this week, not for yesterday.

Time off the farm

We ask the agent when it came in and choose the newest arrival.

Our selection process

From the market floor to your door, in one morning

1

Orders close

Standing orders and changes lock at 18:00 the day before. We add them up into one buying list.

2

We walk the floor

At 05:00 we compare the same item across agents for quality and price before buying anything.

3

Open and check

We open pockets and boxes and check the bottom layer, where problems hide.

4

Sort and pack

Anything marked or soft is taken out while packing. Leaves and herbs travel in cooler boxes.

5

Deliver by morning

Kitchens before 10:00, households by early afternoon, the same day it was bought.

6

Make it right

If something is not good, send a photo that day and we credit it on your next delivery.

Personalised requests

Need something specific? We take special requests with your order:

  • A named variety, size or ripeness (for example, avocados ripe for Saturday)
  • Items not on our list, or out of season
  • Organic or a particular farm's produce
  • Your own mix in a box or combo
  • Bulk or event quantities

How requests are priced

A personalised request may change the price. Special sizes, out-of-season items and small quantities often cost more at the market. We always send you the price first, and we only buy once you say yes.
