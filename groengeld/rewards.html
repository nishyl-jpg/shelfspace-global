Rules

Draw and gift rules, in plain words

  • Open to Groengeld customers aged 18 or older in our delivery areas. Staff and their households may not enter.
  • Entry is automatic with a qualifying paid order. There is no extra cost to enter and prices are not raised to fund prizes.
  • One winner is drawn at random each Monday from the previous week's entries. The draw is overseen and recorded by an independent person.
  • Winners are contacted by WhatsApp or phone. A prize not claimed within 14 days is drawn again.
  • Prizes are as described, cannot be swapped for cash, and may vary by week.
  • We publish only a winner's first name and suburb, and only with their permission.
  • Full rules are available on request and are kept on record.
