How it works

List. Stock. Walk in.

STEP 1

Anyone lists

A brand, supplier or creator adds a product, app or download with its price and terms.

STEP 2

Creators stock and theme

Creators pick what goes on their shelf, set their own code, and style the shop like their channel.

STEP 3

Viewers walk in

Viewers browse the shop or ask Stoavia, and buy without creating an account. The supplier ships the order.
