Content playbook

The product already has an audience: millions have watched these drinks online. Your job is not to explain them. It is to be the Cape Town account that says "they're here, and here's how you get one", inside the rules for alcohol marketing.

Alcohol marketing has rules. Break them and you risk the licence.
  • Before the licence is granted: build the audience and the waiting list. Do not take orders, quote delivery, or run "buy now" messages.
  • Every post and advert carries a clear responsible-drinking line, for example "Not for persons under 18".
  • Nothing that appeals to children: no cartoons, sweets comparisons, school references or under-age-looking people. The industry code expects people shown in alcohol advertising to be, and look, at least 25 verify against the current code.
  • Never show or suggest: drinking and driving, drinking to get drunk, drinking games, downing drinks, alcohol as the reason for social or sexual success, or pregnant women drinking.
  • Anyone paid or given free product to post must label the post clearly as an advert, and their post must carry the responsible-drinking line too. The advertising regulator ruled against a major brewer on exactly this in 2026.
  • Paid adverts must be age-restricted to 18 and over on every platform.
  • Do not use another company's logo or brand name in a way that suggests you are its official seller, unless you are.
Have your attorney read the Advertising Regulatory Board's alcohol code with you once.

2. Account setup, step by step

  1. Instagram Business account; in settings, set minimum age 18 for South Africa.
  2. Facebook Page with the country and age restriction set to 18 and over; link to Instagram.
  3. TikTok account; mark it as a business account; add "18+" to the bio.
  4. WhatsApp Business: greeting message that asks new contacts to confirm they are 18 or older before anything else.
  5. WhatsApp Channel: "Ship in a Bottle: ship's log".
  6. A free email tool with a sign-up form linked from the site; one tick box: "I am 18 or older and want launch news".
  7. Highlights on Instagram: The journey, The range, How delivery works, Trade.
  8. Save a caption footer to paste on every post: "Not for persons under 18. Enjoy responsibly."

6. Paid advertising, once licensed

  • Budget: R5,000 a month, on Facebook and Instagram only, audience aged 21 to 40 in Cape Town (older than the legal minimum on purpose), button "Send WhatsApp message" or "Shop now".
  • Launch month: R30,000 including two paid creator posts, each by an adult creator aged 25 or older whose audience is mostly adult. Ask each for their audience age breakdown before paying. Posts labelled as adverts.
  • Measure cost per first order. A first four-pack earns about R70 to R130 gross, so aim to pay under R60 per new customer and rely on repeat orders.

7. Thirty ready-to-post captions and scripts

Every caption ends with the saved footer: "Not for persons under 18. Enjoy responsibly." Hashtags to rotate: #capetown #capetowndrinks #readytodrink #cocktailscapetown #shipinabottle #importedcocktails

Phase 1 · The voyage (before the licence)

1 · Reel

On screen: "South Africa can't buy the internet's most-filmed cocktail. I'm going to fix that." Voice: "I'm [name], I'm in Cape Town, and this account is the logbook of importing them legally. It takes licences, customs and patience. Follow along."

2 · Post

The logo. "Ship in a Bottle. Because that's what it is: a shipment, in bottles. Waiting list is open, link in bio. 18+ only."

3 · Reel

"What it takes to import alcohol into South Africa, in 30 seconds": company, provincial licence, national registration, customs code, import certificate, label approval. Caption: "Step 1 of 6 done."

4 · Story poll

"Which flavour should be on the first ship?" Four options.

5 · Reel

Tasting samples you bought (you, an adult, seated, glass, ice). "I bought six to taste so you don't waste your first order. Ranking them." Honest ranking.

6 · Post

"Why can't we just sell them now? Because selling alcohol without a licence is illegal, and we plan to be here for years. The list gets first access."

7 · Reel

"How strong is 15%?" Compare one 200 ml cocktail with the alcohol in a standard beer, using labels. "It's a proper drink. Sip it."

8 · Trade post

"Bar and bottle-store owners: want these on your shelf when they land? Trade list is open. WhatsApp us your venue name."

9 · Milestone

"500 on the waiting list. Thank you, Cape Town."

10 · Reel

Lodging the licence application. "Application in. Now we wait, and that's normal."

11 · Post

"While we wait: tell us your suburb in the comments. The most-mentioned areas get delivery first."

12 · Reel

Screen recording of a ship tracker. "That dot is our first pallet."

Phase 2 · Landfall (launch week)

13 · Reel

Opening the first case. On screen: "They've landed." Caption: "Licensed, cleared, labelled, here. Waiting list: check your WhatsApp."

14 · WhatsApp to the waiting list

"Ahoy [name]! Ship in a Bottle is open. You're on the list, so you get first pick before we announce publicly tomorrow. Singles R99, four-packs R360, party cases R2,040. Order here: [link]. ID checked on delivery. Reply STOP to leave the list."

15 · Carousel

"How to order." 1: Pick your pack. 2: Choose flavours. 3: We deliver in Cape Town. 4: Have your ID ready.

16 · Reel

First delivery: driver checks ID at the door (customer's face not shown unless they agree). "Yes, we really check. Every time."

17 · Post

"Flavour [X] sold out in two days. Next ship is already on the water. Join the restock list."

18 · Trade

"Now pouring at @[bar] and on the shelf at @[bottle store]. Stockists: we deliver cases weekly."
