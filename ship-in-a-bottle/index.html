Questions

Good to know

When can I order?

Are these the real imported drinks?

Yes. Everything we sell is the genuine product, imported in its original sealed container, cleared through customs with duties paid, and labelled to South African requirements.

How strong are they?

Most are 15% alcohol by volume in a 200 ml serving, which is stronger than a beer or cider. One is a proper drink. Please treat it like one.

Do you deliver outside Cape Town?

Not at launch. Join the list with your suburb and we will tell you when we reach you.

Can you supply my bar or event?

Yes, if you hold a liquor licence or an event liquor permit. Use the trade button above.

What if a delivery arrives damaged?

Send a photo the same day and we replace or refund it.
